Events in November

Every event is free. Most need a library card, which you can apply for online in five minutes. The access guide lists step-free routes into each room.

Tuesdays, 10:00

Rhyme time

Songs and stories for under-fives and their grown-ups. Groups of more than four should book a place first.

Thursday 13 November, 19:00

The mills of Fenwick

Local historian Agnes Pryce on the four cotton mills that stood on the river. Reserve a seat, as the reading room holds forty.

Saturdays, 14:00

Code club

Build a small game in Scratch, ages 9 to 13. Laptops are provided. Read the club rules with your child before the first session.
