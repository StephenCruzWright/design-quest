The Gull's Rest

A six-room guesthouse on the harbour wall

Sleep beside the harbour at Carrack Bay

The Gull's Rest has stood on the harbour wall since 1888, when it was the harbourmaster's house. Every room faces the water, and on a spring tide the waves break a few metres below the breakfast-room windows.

Breakfast is served from 8:00 to 10:00: local eggs, kippers from the smokehouse on Quay Street and bread baked in our kitchen each morning. We can't take dogs, but we can recommend three places in the village that do.
