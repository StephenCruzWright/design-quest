Lengths

All four strokes, tumble turns and a 100-metre badge at the end of term.

Before your first visit

What should we bring?

A swimsuit, a towel and goggles if your child already has them. We lend floats and fins.

Can I watch?

Yes. The viewing gallery overlooks the teaching pool and has a coffee machine.
